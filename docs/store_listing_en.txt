FULL DESCRIPTION

My First Animals is a friendly little world of games made just for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. Tap adorable animals, hear their real sounds, feed them their favourite food, give them a bath and play simple games designed for small hands. No reading needed, no time limits, no way to fail.

NEW IN 2.0
Every world has been redrawn by hand in one warm cartoon style. The jungle has two brand new games, and there is a whole new fourth world: dinosaurs.

FOUR WORLDS TO EXPLORE

MY FARM
Meet the cow, the horse, the pig, the goat, the chicken and the duckling. Feed them, wash the mud off them, match each animal to its shadow, and find who is hiding behind the haystack.

MY JUNGLE
The monkey, the elephant, the giraffe, the hippo, the lion and the parrot are waiting. Turn over cards to find matching pairs, guess which animal made that sound, swing the monkey from vine to vine to reach a banana, and give every animal a bush of its own size.

MY OCEAN
Pop the rising bubbles to see which little creature is inside. Guide a fish through the coral reef with your finger. Tap sea animals sitting on tall pillars to play a tune. Sort the fish into shells by colour. The ocean is alive even while you are choosing: a swordfish glides past, a jellyfish drifts and puffs, a crab digs into the sand, and there is treasure to tap.

MY DINO
Nothing here is scary. The T-rex is silly, the volcano only smokes, and the baby dinosaurs are glad to see you. Tap an egg until it hatches, lay stones across the lava so a dinosaur can cross, brush the sand away to find bones and put a skeleton together, and stack stones into a tower so a hungry dinosaur can reach the leaves.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
Every game answers a single tap or a single drag. Nothing is timed, nothing is scored, and a wrong answer simply waits for the child to try again. The animals react to every touch, so even the youngest player always gets something back.

REAL SOUNDS
Every animal voice is a real recording, not a synthesised beep, and all of them are levelled so one game is never louder than the next.

FOR PARENTS
Half of the games are free. A single purchase, behind a gate that toddlers cannot open, unlocks the rest in all four worlds. The parents corner also lets you turn the music and sound effects on or off. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.

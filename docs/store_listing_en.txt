Farm, jungle and ocean games for ages 2 to 5. Tap, feed, wash and play!


FULL DESCRIPTION

My First Animals is a friendly little world of games made just for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 2 to 5. Tap adorable animals, hear their real sounds, feed them their favourite food, give them a bath and play simple games designed for small hands. No reading needed, no time limits, no way to fail.

THREE WORLDS TO EXPLORE

MY FARM
Meet the cow, the horse, the pig, the goat, the chicken and the duckling. Feed them, wash the mud off them, match each animal to its shadow, and find who is hiding behind the haystack.

MY JUNGLE
The monkey, the elephant, the giraffe, the hippo, the lion and the parrot are waiting. Turn over cards to find matching pairs, guess which animal made that sound, hand out bananas, and let the elephant shower everyone with its trunk.

MY OCEAN
Brand new in this update, and not a repeat of the other worlds. Pop the rising bubbles to see which little creature is inside. Guide a fish through the coral reef with your finger. Tap sea animals sitting on tall pillars to play a tune. Sort the fish into shells by colour.

The ocean is alive even while you are choosing: a swordfish glides past with a school of fish behind it, a jellyfish drifts and puffs, a crab digs itself into the sand, and there is treasure to tap.

MADE FOR SMALL HANDS
Every game answers a single tap or a single drag. Nothing is timed, nothing is scored, and a wrong answer simply waits for the child to try again. The animals react to every touch, so even the youngest player always gets something back.

REAL SOUNDS
Every animal voice is a real recording, not a synthesised beep, and all of them are levelled so one game is never louder than the next.

FOR PARENTS
A parents corner sits behind a simple gate that toddlers cannot open, where you can turn the music and sound effects on or off.

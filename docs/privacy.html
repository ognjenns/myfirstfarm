Privacy Policy — My First Animals

Oggie Games · Effective date: 27 July 2026

My First Animals is a game for toddlers (ages 2–5) made by Oggie Games. We take children's privacy seriously. The short version: this app does not collect any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information, from children or adults. There are no user accounts, no registration, no chat, and no third-party analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only local settings on your device (for example: music on/off, sound on/off). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The current version of My First Animals contains no advertising.

If a future version introduces ads, they will be strictly limited to non-personalized ads served through ad networks certified for children's apps (compliant with Google Play Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category rules), with no behavioral tracking and no use of advertising identifiers. This policy will be updated before any such change.

In-app purchases

The app offers a single optional one-time purchase ("Unlock all games"), placed behind a parental gate. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play / Apple — we never see or store payment details.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

My First Animals is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR provisions for children. Because the app collects no personal data, no parental consent for data processing is required. External links and purchase options are protected by a parental gate.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@oggiegames.com.

Oggie Games is an independent developer based in Serbia. We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

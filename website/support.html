Purchases & restoring

The optional "Unlock all games" purchase is a one-time payment that unlocks every game, including all future worlds. If you reinstall the app or switch to a new device, open the parents' section (behind the parental gate) and tap Restore purchases — no need to pay again.

Frequently asked

How do I open the parents' section? Tap the small button in the corner of the world screen and answer the math question — it keeps little ones out.

How do I turn music or sounds off? In the parents' section, use the Music and Sounds switches.

Is there any advertising? No. The app has no ads.
